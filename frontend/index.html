Crucial Defensive Distinctions

Understanding the operational boundary between raw data, intelligence indicators, and confirmed incidents.

Concept Defensive Definition Example SOC Analyst Action
OBSERVATION A raw, uncontextualized event or telemetry log captured by security sensors, proxy logs, or firewall tables. Outbound connection on port 443 Ingested into SIEM; no immediate alert triggered without rule threshold match.
INDICATOR (IOC) A specific technical artifact (IP, Domain, URL, Hash) suggesting potential malicious activity with associated intelligence metadata. login-check.invalid Syntactically validated and matched against threat databases for enrichment.
ALERT A prioritized notification generated by a detection rule or correlation engine when telemetry exceeds risk thresholds. Phishing Risk Elevation (Score 78) Triaged in SOC queue; deduplicated to prevent analyst alert fatigue.
THREAT A potential circumstance or actor capability that could exploit vulnerabilities to inflict harm on enterprise assets. Credential Theft Campaign via Spearphishing Mapped to MITRE ATT&CK TTPs; defensive countermeasures prepared.
INCIDENT A confirmed security event that actually compromises confidentiality, integrity, or availability of enterprise systems. Compromised Admin Account & Data Exfiltration Full Incident Response Plan (IRP) initiated: Contain, Eradicate, Recover.
