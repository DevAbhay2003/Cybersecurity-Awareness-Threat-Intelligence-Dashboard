🎣 Anatomy of a Credential Phishing Attack

Inspect the 6 most common tell-tale indicators found in deceptive emails and messages.

Phishing Defense
⚠️ 1. Mismatched Sender Domain

The display name claims to be "Corporate IT Helpdesk", but the actual SMTP address is support@cloud-verify.invalid.

⏰ 2. Artificial High Urgency

"Account suspended in 24 hours!" Urgency exploits panic to bypass rational judgment and security validation.

🔗 3. Deceptive Hyperlink

Hyperlink text reads "https://portal.company.com" but the actual destination points to https://login-check.invalid/auth.

📱 4. QR Code Quishing

Attacker embeds a QR code prompting the victim to scan with a personal phone, bypassing corporate email link filters.

📎 5. Unexpected Attachments

Invoices or payroll spreadsheets delivered as macro-enabled .docm, password-protected .zip, or .iso archive containers.

📣 6. Out-of-Band Verification

Defensive remedy: Always verify unexpected urgent requests via known, independent internal phone numbers or chat.
